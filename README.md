## Reading the notes

- **Source type:** academic papers/preprints, company articles, practitioner accounts, and corporate disclosures are labeled separately.
- **Evidence:** checking a primary source, reading it closely, and reproducing a result are different states. Each note records its own scope.
- **Applications:** practical ideas and research plans are proposals unless explicitly marked as executed and verified.
