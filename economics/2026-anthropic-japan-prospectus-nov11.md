## Governance and agent risks

Printed p.29, PDF p.46, describes the planned governance structure. LTBT’s Class T share generally permits election and removal of directors forming a board majority. Founder LLC’s Class F share is intended to carry 50.1% of votes on general shareholder matters, including election of directors other than Class T directors. Class T and F have no economic rights. Printed pp.125–129 discuss model behavior, limits of monitoring and how errors can propagate in long agent workflows. This is issuer risk disclosure with selected examples. Assessing frequency or general capability requires separately scoped evidence.
