# Anthropic Form S-1/A Amendment No. 1, November 2

## Overview

- **What it is** Anthropic’s first amendment to its US stock registration statement.
- **What it says** Filed November 2, it includes financial and governance disclosures while leaving offering price and share counts blank on the cover.
- **Caveat** This historical preliminary filing has later amendments. Its disclosures need their own dates and accounting periods.

## Practical reading (six lines)

- The document is S-1/A Amendment No. 1.
