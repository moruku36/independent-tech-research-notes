# CrowdStrike’s Zscaler agreement and the proposed Zero Trust integration

## Overview

- **What it is**: A joint corporate announcement about CrowdStrike’s proposed acquisition of Zscaler.
- **What it says**: The companies announce a definitive all-stock agreement and outline a Falcon and Zero Trust integration.
- **Caveat**: Shareholder and regulatory approvals and other closing conditions remained ahead at the announcement.

## Practical reading (six lines)

- CrowdStrike and Zscaler announced the agreement on November 23, 2026.
- The announced enterprise value was approximately USD 43.9 billion using CrowdStrike’s November 20 closing price.
- The exchange ratio was 1.135 CrowdStrike common shares for each Zscaler common share.
- The companies expected closing in the second half of CrowdStrike’s fiscal 2028, subject to conditions.
- The companies describe a plan to bring endpoint and network policy closer together, including for AI agents.
- Proposed use: document current policy ownership and compare it with supported integration features as those are published.

## Source and reading scope

CrowdStrike and Zscaler, November 23, 2026. Checked the joint release and filed SEC exhibit on November 24, 2026 JST. This note records the announced terms and outlines a review of the proposed architecture.

## Evidence and limitations

- **Transaction.** Both boards approved the agreement. Closing requires Zscaler shareholder approval, required regulatory approvals, and other customary conditions. The companies say they will operate independently until closing. The headline valuation depends on the share-price reference date.
- **Integration.** The companies describe combining CrowdStrike Falcon’s endpoint security with Zscaler’s network controls and Zero Trust access. The release presents this as an integration plan and gives forward-looking forecasts. The materials reviewed do not establish a joint deployment schedule, migration requirements, or independently measured outcomes.
