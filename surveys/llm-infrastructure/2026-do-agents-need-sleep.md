# Do agents need to sleep? A testable theory of consolidation

## Overview

- **What it is**: A theory connects biological sleep with agent memory maintenance.
- **What it says**: RRR proposes sampling, coherence selection and renormalization.
- **Caveat**: Predicted superiority remains a hypothesis, not a measured gain.

## Practical reading (six lines)

- Finite memory motivates maintenance during continued learning.
- RRR is mapped conceptually onto digital memories.
- Partial disconnection protects a consolidation pass from changes.
- Our proposal: consolidate a snapshot, then validate promotion.
- Free check: compare fixed synthetic memory policies.
- Do not infer model-weight learning or mandatory full shutdown.

## Question, method, claims and evidence

The authors predict selectionist consolidation will outperform explicit per-entry instruction as memory scales. Biological evidence and cited engineering examples motivate that prediction; they do not constitute a controlled comparison of the proposed unified RRR cycle.

## Comparison with related work

RSPM reports experiments; this paper supplies a different theoretical hypothesis. Do not merge their evidence. [Related note](2026-rspm-self-pruning-memory.md)
