# Maya lidar agents and the correction to a discovery claim

## Overview

- **What it is** Pietro Miotti’s account of agents examining lidar imagery for archaeological structures.
- **What it says** The author’s November 11 update says the site was already mapped in Estrada-Belli et al. 2025.
- **Caveat** This is a corrected practitioner account. Matching the site to the earlier publication has yet to be independently checked for this note.

## Practical reading (six lines)

- The reported workflow converts public lidar strips into terrain images.
- The author describes 48 agents reviewing about 1,200 square kilometres.
- Candidate shapes were checked against raw point clouds.
- A registry reply initially appeared to support the novelty claim.
- The subsequent correction shows why registry status and literature coverage both matter.
- A proposed research workflow keeps a prior-art checklist alongside every discovery claim.

## Source and reading scope

Pietro Miotti, My AI Agents Found an Unmapped Maya Site. Publication date unconfirmed. Checked 2026-11-12 JST, including the November 11 correction and workflow account.

## Method, evidence and limitations

The report describes image review, point-cloud checks and a submission to INAH. The author later says the agents missed an existing publication. There is no controlled detection benchmark or independent novelty assessment in this note.
