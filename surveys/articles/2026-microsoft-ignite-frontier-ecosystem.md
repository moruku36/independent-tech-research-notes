# Ignite 2026: context, governance, models, and enterprise agents

## Overview

- **What it is**: Microsoft's edited Ignite keynote, covering its enterprise AI stack.
- **What it says**: The announcement connects shared work context, model choice, agent management, and security.
- **Caveat**: A vendor roadmap is not an independent performance or deployment evaluation.

## Practical reading (six lines)

- The question is how enterprises turn agent capabilities into controlled business outcomes.
- The keynote links Copilot, Microsoft IQ, Foundry models, Agent 365, and Perception.
- Its emphasis is shared context, organizational control, and independently checkable actions.
- Proposed use: map one workflow's data, model, permissions, owner, and measurable outcome.
- Keep a per-feature availability ledger before planning adoption.
- Product-wide GA does not establish availability of every connector or preview feature.

## Source and reading scope

Microsoft Corporate Blogs, 2026-11-17, “Ignite 2026: Building the frontier ecosystem,” edited remarks by Satya Nadella. Checked 2026-11-18 JST: supplied article in browser, plus the Agent 365 and Intune announcement passages linked below. Primary source partially read; no product deployment or benchmark reproduction.

## Evidence and limitations

The keynote assigns enterprise context to Microsoft IQ, governance/observability to Agent 365, and agentic security to Perception. Licensed MAI weights are access for selected customers, not an announcement of unrestricted open weights. Efficiency claims remain vendor-reported.

The more specific Agent 365 article labels Business Analytics a Frontier preview and several platform connections preview/rolling out. The Intune article labels its Perception capabilities public preview with onboarding conditions. These narrower statements must survive any high-level summary.

## Application and free check

Proposed / untried. Using a fictional document-approval workflow, draw a local ledger of context sources, allowed actions, identities, review evidence, costs, rollback, and acceptance criteria. Compare this map with existing GitHub/Codex practices without connecting a tenant. Confirm region, license, data boundary, feature status, and operational ownership before a later product trial.
