# OpenAI's commitments and policy proposals for AI compute campuses

## Overview

- Company policy article on local impacts of US AI compute campuses and OpenAI's commitments.
- It outlines paying for power, reporting water use and noise, local jobs, and community participation.
- Most points are future commitments or policy proposals, not measured outcomes or independent audits.

## Practical reading (7 lines)

- OpenAI says each future campus it owns and operates will have a local compact.
- It commits to covering electricity and related infrastructure costs, with financial assurances if a project changes.
- It promises site-level water and electricity reporting plus construction plans and noise studies.
- It describes early resident input and opportunities for local workers and businesses.
- Separately, it asks policymakers for rules on utility costs, water, NDAs, and tax-incentive transparency.
- A practical review should track which campuses are covered, metrics, reporting cadence, and external verification.
- The article states current policy and proposals rather than demonstrated results.

## Source and verification scope

## Limitations and open questions
