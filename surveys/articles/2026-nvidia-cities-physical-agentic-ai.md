## Practical reading (7 lines)

- The article connects city cameras, sensors, and service data with resident support and operations.
- It covers service agents, video search and summarization, and planning through digital twins.
- It describes starting with small GPU systems and sharing infrastructure across departments.
- ITMAX's support for more than 17,000 cameras is a 2027 target, not a completed result.
- Proposed application: measure turnaround, incorrect answers, and staff handoffs for one municipal workflow.

## Research question

How can cities use existing data and computing assets to expand resident services, video operations, and infrastructure planning?

## Method, data, and comparison conditions

The article surveys resident services in Spain, shared video infrastructure in Poland, traffic management in Malaysia, and planning in Taiwan. It presents vendor and partner cases rather than a controlled comparison.

## Claims and evidence

Examples include 1MillionBot deployments, K2K infrastructure in Wrocław, and ITMAX vision agents. Keep the reported deployments distinct from ITMAX's 2027 target and the planned digital twin at Dangote's refinery.

## Limitations and threats to validity

Cases are selected by the vendor. The article provides no common evaluation of comparison conditions, sample sizes, costs, false detections, or privacy impacts. This note has not verified causal links between reported improvements and public outcomes.
