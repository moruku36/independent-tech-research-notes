# Anthropic’s Claude Forward Deployed Engineer learning path

## Overview

- Anthropic Partner Academy’s self-paced path for software builders who work directly with customer teams.
- Eleven courses cover Claude foundations, development and pilot delivery. The stated outcome is a plan for a customer pilot.
- The evidence is the provider's syllabus and program pages. Access requires Claude Partner Network membership, and the linked certification has a separate exam.

## Six practical takeaways

- The syllabus lists 11 courses and estimates about 9.5 hours of self-paced study.
- The path shares nine courses with the Claude Developer path. The two remaining delivery courses take about 100 minutes in total.
- Development topics include Claude Platform, Claude Code, permission modes, hooks and verification, Model Context Protocol (MCP), and reusable Skills.
- A pilot plan sets the current baseline, defines success measures in advance, selects a first release scope, and describes onboarding for the first users.
- The path points to separate preparation and registration for Claude Certified Developer – Foundations. Frontier Academy’s Frontier Deployed Engineer Residency has its own practical training and assessment.
- Proposed application. Draft a one-page pilot plan for an open or synthetic workload and specify who owns measurement, onboarding and the decision to expand.

## Publication and review scope

Anthropic Partner Academy, Claude Forward Deployed Engineer path, checked November 26, 2026 JST. Reviewed the path outline and Anthropic’s related program descriptions. Individual lessons, enrollment and assessments were not tested.

## Evidence and limitations

- The four foundation items cover capabilities and limitations, AI fluency, platform and model foundations, and choosing a model. Five building courses cover the API/platform workflow, two Claude Code courses, MCP, and Skills.
- Delivery Methodology introduces use-case qualification, baselining, pilot design and risk. Driving Enterprise Adoption of Claude covers team roles, launch, enablement and the next workload. These are advertised topics. The outline provides no measured effect on deployment outcomes.
- The path recommends the Foundations certification prep and exam. The Frontier Academy separately describes a four-day intensive, 12-week residency and practical assessment for its credential.

## Application and low-cost check

Proposed and untried. Pick one public or synthetic workflow. Record its current completion time and quality, a target agreed before implementation, the initial user group, and when to review whether the pilot met its target.
