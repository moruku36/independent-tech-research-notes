# Jane Street’s Socratic Tutor skill design

## Overview

- **What it is** A public instruction file for structured Socratic tutoring.
- **What it says** It specifies diagnostic placement, subskill levels, questioning, spaced recall and a final assessment.
- **Caveat** The file defines a workflow. It supplies no controlled evidence of learning effectiveness.

## Practical reading (six lines)

- It is intended for explicit requests for sustained tutoring.
- A short diagnostic places the learner on a subskill map.
- The teaching loop asks one question at a time.
- Level increases require independent checks including recall.
- A proposed trial measures delayed retention as well as immediate answers.
- The note examines the design as source material. It has not been installed or trialed.

## Content, evidence and limitations

The design distinguishes short explanations from an ongoing teaching session. It includes rechecks and commands for adjusting difficulty or saving state. No learner study or validated mastery scale accompanies the inspected file.
