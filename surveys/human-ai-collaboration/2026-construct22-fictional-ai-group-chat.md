# Construct22: a fictional group chat as AI-generated self-portrait

## Overview

- A public page showcasing a playful prompt for a fictional group chat about the person interacting with an assistant.
- The prompt asks the assistant to draw on what it knows about the user and present the result as dialogue between named AI systems.
- The page presents a generated scene and one edited-for-publication example. The scene supplies no evidence of actual inter-model communication.

## Six practical takeaways

- The prompt frames a personalized self-portrait as a WhatsApp-style conversation among fictional versions of several assistants.
- The creator uses recognizable habits and playful exaggeration to make the output feel familiar.
- The page labels the sample fictional. It documents one output shared with permission, translated from Turkish and with identifying details generalized.
- Familiar details may draw on context or inference. The page presents one generated example as its evidence.
- The page offers a creative format, without a systematic comparison across models or a test of memory and privacy behavior.
- Proposed activity. Use a fictional profile and mark which jokes came from given details and which were invented.

## Publication and review scope

Construct22, public project page, reviewed on November 28, 2026 JST. The page's description and published example were checked. Authorship and publication date were not established. No independent prompt run.

## Evidence and limitations

- The title and introduction use playful language about revealing hidden opinions, while the page describes the group as invented. Model and service names are characters in the generated text.
- The creator says the example is a translated and anonymized output shared with permission. It is a single selected example and offers no controlled evaluation of recall, factual accuracy, or real system behavior.
- Recognition of a personalized detail is a reader response. Establishing where a detail came from would require a controlled input and evidence of the information available to that run.

## Application and low-cost check

Proposed and untried. Supply a short invented profile to one assistant. Review the resulting fictional dialogue against the profile, labeling recognizable details, plausible inferences and inventions.
